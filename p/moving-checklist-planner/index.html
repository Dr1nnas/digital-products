Description

Move home without the chaos. This 18-page printable planner takes you step by step from eight weeks before the move to your first month in the new home, so you only ever need to look at one short list.

What's inside (18 pages, A4 and US Letter)

  • Move Overview: addresses, key dates, key contacts and budget summary
  • Moving Timeline (3 pages): 8, 6, 4 and 2 weeks before, 1 week before, 1–2 days before, moving day, first week and first month. That's 54 pre-written tasks plus blank lines for your own.
  • Change of Address: 18 common organisations already listed
  • Utilities & Services: stop/start dates, account numbers and meter readings for both homes
  • Mover Quotes: compare three quotes side by side, with questions to ask
  • Moving Budget: estimate vs actual
  • Declutter: sell, donate, give away or recycle
  • Packing Plan by Room, with packing tips
  • Essentials Box: everything for the first night
  • Box Inventory
  • Move-Out Cleaning checklist
  • Moving Day Plan: hour by hour
  • New Home: First Week checklist
  • Notes & Contacts
  • Cover and how-to-use page

Works anywhere

The tasks are written for any country: no country-specific forms, just the jobs every move needs. Cross out what doesn't apply and add your own.

Good to know

  • Digital download only. Nothing is shipped.
  • Fixed-text PDF: the pages are not editable. Print and write, or annotate in a PDF note-taking app.
  • General guidance only, not legal or financial advice.
  • Personal use only. Please don't share or resell the files.
