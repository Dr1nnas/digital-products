Description

Plan the week's meals in ten minutes, shop once and waste less. This undated kit gives you 13 clean, ink-light pages, from the weekly menu to the grocery list, pantry and freezer inventory, recipe cards and a food budget.

What's inside (13 pages, A4 and US Letter)

  • Weekly Meal Plan: breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks for 7 days, plus Prep ahead and Remember to buy boxes
  • Dinners & Shopping: one dinner panel per day, with a shopping list beside it
  • Grocery List by aisle: produce, dairy & eggs, meat & fish, bakery, pantry, frozen, drinks, household, other
  • Monthly Meal Plan: undated 5-week grid
  • Meal Prep Planner: batch-cook table, two prep-day task lists and a use-up-first list
  • Pantry Inventory and Fridge & Freezer pages with date-in and use-by columns
  • Meal Ideas & Favourites: your go-to meals in one place
  • Recipe Cards: two per page
  • Food Budget Tracker: planned vs spent
  • Weekly Kitchen Reset: a 30-minute checklist
  • Cover and how-to-use page

How it works

Download, then print the pages you need at home or at a print shop, as many times as you like. The pages are undated, so you can start any week. You can also write on them digitally in a note-taking app that supports PDF annotation.

Good to know

  • Digital download only. Nothing is shipped.
  • Fixed-text PDF: the pages are not editable.
  • Colours may vary slightly between screens and printers.
  • Personal use only. Please don't share or resell the files.
