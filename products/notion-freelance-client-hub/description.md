# Freelance Client & Project Hub (Notion)

Run your freelance business from one place. This **Notion import pack** gives you six linked databases for clients, projects, tasks, time, invoices and leads, a Home dashboard, and the routines that keep a one-person business on track.

![Freelance Client & Project Hub preview](preview.png)

## What's inside

- **Clients**: status, contact, email, phone, hourly rate, billing type, source, services, payment terms
- **Projects**: client, status, type, pricing, start, deadline, budget, estimated hours
- **Tasks**: project, status, priority, due date, tag, estimate
- **Time Log**: date, project, hours, billable and invoiced
- **Invoices**: number, client, project, amount, issued, due, paid status, method
- **Leads / Pipeline**: stage, value, probability, follow-ups, link to the client once won
- **18 formulas** to paste in: hours logged, hours left, progress bar, effective hourly rate, deadline countdown, billable amount, overdue flags, payment status, weighted pipeline value
- **17 recommended views**: project board, timeline, deadlines calendar, today & overdue, this week's hours, unbilled time, unpaid invoices, pipeline board
- **Pages**: Home dashboard, Client Onboarding Checklist, Project Kickoff Template, Weekly Review, Rate Calculator Notes, How to Set Up
- **110 rows of linked sample data**, plus blank (headers-only) versions of every database

## How it works

1. Import the pages zip in Notion (Settings › Import › Text & Markdown).
2. Import the six CSVs in order: Clients, Projects, Tasks, Time Log, Invoices, Leads.
3. Follow the guide to switch on relations, paste the formulas and add the views (about 20–30 minutes).
