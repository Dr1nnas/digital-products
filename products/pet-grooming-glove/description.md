# Pet Hair Removal Mitt for Furniture and Clothes

A flat, oval mitt with a textured mesh surface. Slip your hand under the band and wipe in one direction to gather pet hair and lint from fabric.

## Features
- Textured mesh surface grabs hair and fluff
- The band across the back holds it on your hand
- approx. 24 x 16 cm
- Reusable: shake or rinse clean, no sticky refills
- For sofas, beds, car seats, cushions and clothes

## Good to know
This mitt is for cleaning fabrics, not for brushing your pet. Sold as 1 piece in brown with light-grey edging.
